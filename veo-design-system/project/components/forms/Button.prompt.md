Square-cornered VEO button for every action; use `signal` (Lime) for the single most important CTA in a view.

```jsx
<Button variant="signal" icon={<Glyph name="arrow-up-right" size={16} />}>Hazlo ahora</Button>
<Button variant="primary" fullWidth>Quiero ver mi marca puesta</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" size="sm">Descargar reporte</Button>
```

- Variants: primary (Dark), signal (Lime), secondary (outline), inverse (white, for dark fields), ghost, danger.
- Sizes: sm 36 · md 48 · lg 60. Labels are Funnel Sans Regular, sentence case.
- Arrow ↗ (`arrow-up-right`) is the brand's CTA mark.
