Square icon-only button for toolbars, close actions and the lime ↗ tile.

```jsx
<IconButton label="Cerrar" icon={<Glyph name="x" />} />
<IconButton label="Ir" variant="signal" size="lg" icon={<Glyph name="arrow-up-right" />} />
```

- Variants: ghost (default), primary, signal, secondary, inverse (on dark).
